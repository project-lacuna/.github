# Governance

## Baseline authority

Project Lacuna, LLC maintains organization-wide repository safety requirements in [POLICY.md](POLICY.md). Repository maintainers are responsible for applying those requirements in their repositories and may impose stricter controls.

## Decision principles

* Prefer the least privilege and smallest practical blast radius.
* Prefer reversible, reviewable changes over irreversible automation.
* Keep decisions proportionate to the data, system, financial, and reputational impact involved.
* Record exceptions and material technical decisions in the appropriate private governance location.

## Exceptions

An exception to an organization baseline must be documented, time-bounded, owned by an authorized decision-maker, and reviewed before expiry. Exceptions must identify the requirement, reason, risk, compensating controls, owner, and expiration date.
