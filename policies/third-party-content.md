# Third-Party Content Baseline

## Scope

This policy applies to external code, documentation, prompts, datasets, examples, media, benchmarks, model artifacts, and generated material introduced into organization repositories.

## Requirements

- Record a source URL or other provenance for material that is not trivial or wholly original.
- Check and comply with applicable license, attribution, notice, and reuse conditions.
- Do not assume public availability grants permission for redistribution, commercial use, training, fine-tuning, or inclusion in a product.
- Do not import material with unknown or incompatible rights.
- Keep material with personal, customer, confidential, or restricted content out of repositories unless explicitly approved under a repository-specific policy.
- Review AI-generated material for likely copying, inaccurate attribution, security defects, and suitability before merge.
