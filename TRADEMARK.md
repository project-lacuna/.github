All trademark rights are reserved except as expressly granted in writing.

### Permitted referential use

Nominative or referential use of the `LACUNA` mark is permitted only to the limited extent reasonably necessary to make truthful statements about compatibility, origin, commentary, criticism, scholarship, or other lawful descriptive reference, provided that such use does not create a likelihood of confusion as to source, sponsorship, or affiliation.

Examples of generally permissible referential use include:

* Identifying an unmodified official release as originating from Project Lacuna, LLC
* Stating that a third-party tool, supplement, or integration is "for LACUNA" or "compatible with LACUNA"
* Referring to Project Lacuna, LLC or the `LACUNA` brand in commentary, news reporting, comparative discussion, or criticism

Any such use must be proportional, accurate, and accompanied where appropriate by a clear statement that the material is unofficial and not endorsed by Project Lacuna, LLC.

### Prohibited uses absent written permission

Absent prior written permission from Project Lacuna, LLC, the following uses are prohibited:

* Use of `LACUNA` or any confusingly similar designation in the title or branding of a product, game, publication, service, repository, organization, or business
* Use of official logos, stylized marks, or trade dress
* Use of the mark in a manner likely to cause consumer confusion, mistake, deception, or implied endorsement
* Rebranding forks, derivatives, adaptations, or companion tools as official `LACUNA` products
* Use of the mark in connection with merchandise, advertising, promotional materials, crowdfunding, or storefront listings in a manner implying authorization

## Community projects, forks, and derivative works

Where an underlying content or code license permits copying, modification, or redistribution, community projects and forks may exist, but they must adopt branding that is clearly distinct from Project Lacuna, LLC.

At a minimum, any such project must:

* Use a distinct project name
* Use a distinct logo and visual identity
* Avoid presenting itself as an official Project Lacuna, LLC offering
* Include a clear and conspicuous statement that the project is unofficial
* Limit use of the `LACUNA` mark to truthful referential statements only
